Be My Image

A browser extension that scans the current web page for images, captures full pages (including infinite feeds), and lets you edit and save the results — entirely on your own computer. Nothing you scan, capture, or edit ever leaves your device.

Copyright notice

Download only images that you own or have permission to use. Be My Image does not bypass login restrictions, paywalls, DRM, or website access controls.

Data deletion & requests

Be My Image stores data only on your device (settings, and images you chose to keep in the Studio). There is no server-side account and no server-side data, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. To remove everything locally:

  • Use Clear in the Studio to empty the collected list.
  • Uninstall the extension — the browser then removes its local storage entirely.

If you believe the extension holds any data about you elsewhere, contact admin@danielsword.com and we will respond to your request.
