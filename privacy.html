Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Be My Image processes webpage images locally on the user's device. Downloaded images, webpage content, browsing history, and page URLs are not uploaded to any server. Image scanning begins only after the user initiates it.

What the extension does with data

  • All scanning, capturing, editing, and saving happens locally in your browser.
  • No image, page content, URL, or usage data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • There are no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and no remote code.
  • Scanning runs only on the page you are viewing, and only after you click a button.

What is stored, and where

DataWhereWhy
Your settings (file formats, sizes, save folder, capture options)Browser extension storage on your device (synced by your browser if you use profile sync)So your preferences persist
Captured / collected images you keep in the StudioBrowser local storage (IndexedDB) on your deviceSo the Studio can reopen your session

Nothing above ever leaves your device via the extension.

Donations and feedback

The Donate button only opens an external payment page (e.g. PayPal) in a new tab. The extension itself never collects, sees, or stores any payment information. The in-app feedback form sends only the text you typed (plus the extension version and browser version, to help reproduce issues) to the developer's inbox via the Web3Forms delivery service — no account, sender address, or any other data is required or collected. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Data deletion

Use Clear in the Studio to remove collected images, or uninstall the extension to remove all of its local data. Because nothing is stored server-side, no further request is needed — but you can always reach us at admin@danielsword.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
